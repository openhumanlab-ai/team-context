Three lines. If you need more, you're writing reasoning, and reasoning goes stale in a week.

Chose: the thing. One sentence.
Ruled out: the alternatives, with a few words each on why not. This is the line that saves the next person from re-litigating.
Revisit if: the condition that would reopen this. A number, a date, an event. Without this line the decision becomes a rule nobody can question.
Owner: who to ask. A name, not a team.

Retires: "old sentence" "another"
